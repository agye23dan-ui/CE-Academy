# Physics I (Mechanics and Waves): Class Test

Duration: 60 minutes. Calculator allowed. Use g = 9.8 m/s^2 and v_sound = 343 m/s. Total: 60 marks. Section A: 20 multiple-choice questions (1 mark each). Section B: 4 problems (10 marks each).

## Section A: Multiple choice (20 marks)

**1.** A ball is thrown straight up at 20 m/s. The time to reach the highest point is about
A) 1.0 s  B) 2.0 s  C) 4.0 s  D) 20 s

**2.** A car accelerates uniformly from rest to 20 m/s in 5 s. The distance travelled is
A) 20 m  B) 50 m  C) 100 m  D) 200 m

**3.** On level ground and neglecting air resistance, the launch angle giving maximum range is
A) 30°  B) 45°  C) 60°  D) 90°

**4.** A net force of 10 N acts on a 2 kg object. Its acceleration is
A) 0.2 m/s^2  B) 5 m/s^2  C) 12 m/s^2  D) 20 m/s^2

**5.** The weight of a 5 kg object on Earth is about
A) 5 N  B) 9.8 N  C) 49 N  D) 0.51 N

**6.** A block slides down a frictionless 30° incline. Its acceleration is
A) 9.8 m/s^2  B) 8.5 m/s^2  C) 4.9 m/s^2  D) 0

**7.** The work done lifting a 10 kg mass 2 m at constant speed is about
A) 20 J  B) 98 J  C) 196 J  D) 2000 J

**8.** The kinetic energy of a 2 kg object moving at 3 m/s is
A) 3 J  B) 6 J  C) 9 J  D) 18 J

**9.** A spring with k = 100 N/m is stretched 0.20 m. The stored energy is
A) 2 J  B) 4 J  C) 10 J  D) 20 J

**10.** Total momentum is conserved
A) only in elastic collisions  B) in all collisions when external forces are negligible  C) only in inelastic collisions  D) never in collisions

**11.** A moving ball makes a head-on elastic collision with an identical stationary ball. Afterwards
A) both move at half speed  B) the first stops and the second moves with the original speed  C) both stop  D) the first rebounds

**12.** The moment of inertia of a thin hoop of mass M and radius R about its axis is
A) (1/2) M R^2  B) (2/5) M R^2  C) M R^2  D) (1/12) M R^2

**13.** A torque of 3 N m acts on a body with I = 0.5 kg m^2. The angular acceleration is
A) 1.5 rad/s^2  B) 3.5 rad/s^2  C) 6 rad/s^2  D) 0.17 rad/s^2

**14.** Angular momentum of a system is conserved when
A) kinetic energy is constant  B) the net external torque is zero  C) the net external force is zero only  D) the speed is constant

**15.** If a satellite's orbital radius doubles, its period increases by a factor of
A) 2  B) 2.83  C) 4  D) 8

**16.** If the mass on a spring is quadrupled, the period
A) quadruples  B) doubles  C) halves  D) is unchanged

**17.** If the length of a simple pendulum is quadrupled, the period
A) quadruples  B) doubles  C) halves  D) is unchanged

**18.** A wave has frequency 100 Hz and wavelength 0.5 m. Its speed is
A) 50 m/s  B) 200 m/s  C) 0.005 m/s  D) 100.5 m/s

**19.** The fundamental standing wave on a string fixed at both ends of length L has wavelength
A) L/2  B) L  C) 2L  D) 4L

**20.** Increasing sound intensity by a factor of 10 raises the intensity level by
A) 1 dB  B) 3 dB  C) 10 dB  D) 100 dB

## Section B: Problems (40 marks)

**B1. (10 marks)** A 5.0 kg box is pulled across a floor (μ_k = 0.20) by a 20 N force directed 30° above the horizontal. Starting from rest: (a) find the normal force and friction force [3]; (b) find the acceleration [2]; (c) find the speed and distance after 4.0 s [2]; (d) verify the work-energy theorem over those 4 s by computing the work of the pull, the work of friction and the change in kinetic energy [3].

**B2. (10 marks)** A 0.20 kg ball moving at +5.0 m/s collides head-on with a 0.30 kg ball moving at -2.0 m/s. (a) If the collision is perfectly elastic, find both final velocities and check the conservation laws [6]. (b) If it is perfectly inelastic, find the common velocity and the kinetic energy lost [4].

**B3. (10 marks)** A 1.0 kg mass hangs from a light string wrapped around a uniform solid disc of mass 4.0 kg and radius 0.50 m, free to rotate about its axis. The mass is released from rest. (a) Find the acceleration of the mass and the string tension [5]. (b) Find the angular acceleration of the disc [2]. (c) Find the speed of the mass after falling 2.0 m, using energy conservation as a check [3].

**B4. (10 marks)** (a) A 0.50 kg mass on a spring with k = 50 N/m oscillates with amplitude 0.10 m. Find the angular frequency, period, maximum speed, total energy, and the kinetic and potential energies at x = 0.060 m [6]. (b) A 1.2 m string fixed at both ends has a fundamental frequency of 100 Hz. Find the wave speed, and the tension if μ = 0.0050 kg/m [4].

### Section B

**B1.** Take m = 5.0 kg, F = 20 N, θ = 30°.
(a) Vertical balance: N + F sin θ = m g, so N = 49 - 10 = **39.0 N**. Friction f_k = μ_k N = 0.20 · 39.0 = **7.8 N**. (1 mark diagram or equations, 1 each value.)
(b) Horizontal: F cos θ - f_k = m a, so a = (17.32 - 7.8)/5 = **1.90 m/s^2**.
(c) v = a t = 1.904 · 4 = **7.62 m/s**. d = (1/2) a t^2 = 0.5 · 1.904 · 16 = **15.2 m**.
(d) W_pull = F cos θ · d = 17.32 · 15.23 = **263.8 J**. W_friction = -7.8 · 15.23 = **-118.8 J**. (Normal force, weight: zero work.) Net W = 145.0 J. ΔK = (1/2)(5)(7.616)^2 = **145.0 J** ✓.

**B2.**
(a) Step 1: m1 = 0.20, v1 = 5.0; m2 = 0.30, v2 = -2.0.
Step 2: Total momentum p = 1.0 - 0.6 = 0.4 kg m/s.
Step 3: Elastic: v1' = [(m1 - m2) v1 + 2 m2 v2]/(m1 + m2) = [(-0.1)(5) + 2(0.3)(-2)]/0.5 = (-0.5 - 1.2)/0.5 = **-3.4 m/s**. v2' = [2 m1 v1 + (m2 - m1) v2]/(m1 + m2) = [2(0.2)(5) + (0.1)(-2)]/0.5 = (2 - 0.2)/0.5 = **+3.6 m/s**.
Step 4: Checks: momentum 0.2(-3.4) + 0.3(3.6) = -0.68 + 1.08 = 0.4 ✓; kinetic energy before: 2.5 + 0.6 = 3.1 J; after: 1.156 + 1.944 = 3.1 J ✓; relative speed before 7.0, after 3.6 - (-3.4) = 7.0 ✓.
(b) Step 1: v' = p/(m1 + m2) = 0.4/0.5 = **0.80 m/s**.
Step 2: K after = (1/2)(0.5)(0.64) = 0.16 J.
Step 3: **Energy lost = 3.1 - 0.16 = 2.94 J** (95%).

**B3.** Let M = 4.0 kg, R = 0.50 m, I = (1/2) M R^2 = 0.50 kg m^2, m = 1.0 kg.
(a) Step 1: For the mass: m g - T = m a.
Step 2: For the disc: T R = I α = I a/R, so T = (1/2) M a.
Step 3: Then m g = (m + M/2) a, so **a = 9.8/3 = 3.27 m/s^2**, and T = (1/2)(4)(3.267) = **6.53 N**.
Step 4: Check: m(g - a) = 6.53 ✓.
(b) α = a/R = **6.53 rad/s^2**.
(c) v = sqrt(2 a d) = sqrt(2 · 3.267 · 2) = **3.61 m/s**. Energy check: m g h = (1/2) m v^2 + (1/2) I ω^2 = (1/2)(1)v^2 + (1/4)(4) v^2 = 1.5 v^2, so v^2 = 19.6/1.5 = 13.07 and v = 3.61 m/s ✓.

**B4.**
(a) ω = sqrt(k/m) = sqrt(100) = **10 rad/s**; T = 2π/ω = **0.628 s**; v_max = A ω = **1.0 m/s**; E = (1/2) k A^2 = (1/2)(50)(0.01) = **0.25 J**. At x = 0.060: U = (1/2)(50)(0.0036) = **0.090 J**; K = E - U = **0.160 J** (so v = 0.80 m/s).
(b) f1 = v/(2L), so v = 2 L f1 = 2(1.2)(100) = **240 m/s**. v = sqrt(T/μ), so T = μ v^2 = 0.0050 · 57,600 = **288 N**.
