# Physics II (Electricity and Magnetism): Class Test

Duration: 60 minutes. Calculator allowed. Constants: k = 8.99 × 10^9 N m^2/C^2, ε0 = 8.854 × 10^-12 F/m, e = 1.602 × 10^-19 C, m_e = 9.109 × 10^-31 kg, μ0 = 4π × 10^-7 T m/A. Total: 60 marks. Section A: 20 multiple-choice questions (1 mark each). Section B: 4 problems (10 marks each).

## Section B: Problems (40 marks)

**B1. (10 marks)** Charges +5.0 nC and -5.0 nC are on the x-axis at x = 0 and x = 0.20 m. (a) Find the electric field at the midpoint [3]. (b) Find the potential at the midpoint [2]. (c) Find the work needed to bring a +1.0 nC test charge from infinity to the midpoint [2]. (d) Find the potential energy of the two-charge system [3].

**B2. (10 marks)** A solid conducting sphere of radius 0.10 m carries +10 nC. Find (a) the electric field at 0.20 m [2]; (b) the potential at the surface and at 0.20 m [3]; (c) the surface charge density and the field just outside the surface [3]; (d) the field inside [2].

**B3. (10 marks)** A 24 V ideal source feeds a 6 Ω resistor in series with a parallel pair of 12 Ω and 6 Ω resistors. (a) Find the total resistance and current [3]. (b) Find the current and power in each resistor [4]. (c) A 1.0 mF capacitor charged to 24 V is discharged through a 6 Ω resistor; find the time constant and the time for the voltage to fall to 6 V [3].

**B4. (10 marks)** (a) An electron moves at 1.0 × 10^6 m/s perpendicular to a 2.0 mT field. Find the radius of its path [2]. (b) Find B inside a solenoid with 1000 turns/m carrying 3.0 A [2]. (c) A 50-turn coil of area 0.020 m^2 sits in a field perpendicular to its plane that falls from 0.40 T to zero in 0.20 s. Find the induced EMF, and the current if the coil resistance is 4.0 Ω, and explain the direction using Lenz's law [4]. (d) A series RL circuit with R = 30 Ω and X_L = 40 Ω is connected to 100 V rms: find |Z|, the current, the average power and the power factor [2].

### Section B

**B1.**
(a) At the midpoint (r = 0.10 m from each) the field from +5.0 nC points away from it (toward +x, towards the negative charge) and the field from -5.0 nC points toward it (also toward +x), so the fields add: E = 2 k q/r^2 = 2 · 8.99 × 10^9 · 5.0 × 10^-9/0.010 = **8990 N/C directed toward the negative charge (+x)**.
(b) V = k q/r + k(-q)/r = **0 V**.
(c) W = q_test (V_midpoint - V_∞) = 1.0 × 10^-9 · 0 = **0 J**.
(d) U = k q1 q2/d = 8.99 × 10^9 · (5.0 × 10^-9)(-5.0 × 10^-9)/0.20 = **-1.12 μJ** (negative: the system is bound, and work must be done to separate the charges).

**B2.**
(a) Outside, the sphere behaves as a point charge: E = k Q/r^2 = 8.99 × 10^9 · 10^-8/0.040 = **2250 N/C** (radially outward).
(b) V(surface) = k Q/R = 89.9/0.10 = **899 V**; V(0.20 m) = 89.9/0.20 = **449 V**.
(c) σ = Q/(4π R^2) = 10^-8/(0.1257) = **7.96 × 10^-8 C/m^2**; E just outside = σ/ε0 = **8990 N/C** (check: k Q/R^2 = 8990 ✓).
(d) **E = 0** inside a conductor in equilibrium (and V = 899 V throughout).

**B3.**
(a) The parallel pair: (12 · 6)/18 = 4 Ω. R_total = 6 + 4 = **10 Ω**; I = 24/10 = **2.4 A**.
(b) Step 1: The 6 Ω series resistor: I = 2.4 A, P = I^2 R = 5.76 · 6 = **34.6 W**.
Step 2: The voltage across the pair = 2.4 · 4 = 9.6 V: the 12 Ω carries 9.6/12 = **0.80 A** (P = 7.68 W) and the other 6 Ω carries 9.6/6 = **1.60 A** (P = 15.4 W).
Step 3: Total power = 34.6 + 7.68 + 15.4 = 57.6 W = 24 V · 2.4 A ✓.
(c) τ = R C = 6 · 10^-3 = **6.0 ms**. v = 24 e^(-t/τ) = 6, so e^(-t/τ) = 0.25 and t = τ ln 4 = **8.3 ms**.

**B4.**
(a) r = m v/(e B) = 9.109 × 10^-31 · 10^6/(1.602 × 10^-19 · 2.0 × 10^-3) = **2.84 mm**.
(b) B = μ0 n I = 4π × 10^-7 · 1000 · 3.0 = **3.77 mT**.
(c) |ε| = N A ΔB/Δt = 50 · 0.020 · 0.40/0.20 = **2.0 V**; I = ε/R = 2.0/4.0 = **0.50 A**. The flux (into the page, say) is decreasing, so by Lenz's law the induced current flows in the direction that produces a field **in the same direction** as the original, opposing the decrease.
(d) |Z| = sqrt(30^2 + 40^2) = **50 Ω**; I = 100/50 = **2.0 A**; P = I^2 R = 4 · 30 = **120 W**; power factor = R/|Z| = **0.60**.
