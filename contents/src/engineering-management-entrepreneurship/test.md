## Section B: Problems (40 marks)

**B1. Time value of money (10 marks)** (a) Find the value after 4 years of $5,000 at 10 percent per year [2]. (b) Find the present value of $1,200 paid at the end of each year for 6 years at 8 percent [3]. (c) Find the effective annual rate for 12 percent nominal compounded quarterly [2]. (d) Explain the difference between nominal and real interest rates [3].

**B2. Project evaluation and break-even (10 marks)** (a) A project costs $30,000 and returns $10,000 a year for 4 years; MARR = 10 percent. Compute the NPV, the payback and say whether to accept (the IRR is 12.6 percent) [5]. (b) A product has fixed cost $60,000, price $25 and variable cost $15. Find the break-even volume, and profit at 8,000 units [5].

**B3. Project control (10 marks)** (a) Tasks (weeks): A 2 (none); B 4 (A); C 3 (A); D 2 (B, C). Compute the earliest start and finish, the critical path, the duration and the float of C [6]. (b) BAC = $100,000, PV = $50,000, EV = $40,000, AC = $50,000: compute CPI, SPI and EAC = BAC/CPI [4].

**B4. Venture economics (10 marks)** (a) A subscription has ARPU $20/month, 75 percent gross margin, churn 5 percent/month and CAC $100. Compute LTV, LTV/CAC and the CAC payback [5]. (b) A startup has $150,000 in cash and burns $25,000 per month: what is the runway? [2] (c) An investor puts $500,000 into a company at a $1.5 million pre-money valuation: what is the post-money valuation and the investor's share? [3]

### Section B

**B1.**
(a) FV = 5,000 × 1.1⁴ = **$7,320.50** [2].
(b) PV = 1,200 × [1 - 1.08⁻⁶]/0.08 = 1,200 × 4.6229 = **$5,547** [3].
(c) EAR = (1 + 0.12/4)⁴ - 1 = 1.03⁴ - 1 = **12.55 percent** [2].
(d) The nominal rate is the stated rate in current money; the real rate adjusts for inflation: real = (1 + nominal)/(1 + inflation) - 1 (for example 10 percent nominal with 4 percent inflation gives 5.77 percent real). Compare cash flows consistently: nominal cash flows with a nominal discount rate, or real with real [3].

**B2.**
(a) NPV = -30,000 + 10,000 × 3.1699 = **+$1,699** [2]; payback = 30,000/10,000 = **3.0 years** [1]; NPV > 0 (and IRR 12.6 percent exceeds the MARR of 10 percent), so **accept** [2].
(b) Break-even = 60,000/(25 - 15) = **6,000 units** [2]; profit at 8,000 units = 10 × 8,000 - 60,000 = **$20,000** [3].

**B3.**
(a) A: ES 0, EF 2. B: ES 2, EF 6. C: ES 2, EF 5. D: ES 6 (waits for B), EF 8 [3]. Critical path **A-B-D = 2 + 4 + 2 = 8 weeks** [1]. C's latest finish is 6, so its float is 6 - 5 = **1 week** [2].
(b) CPI = 40,000/50,000 = **0.80**; SPI = 40,000/50,000 = **0.80**; EAC = 100,000/0.80 = **$125,000** [4].

**B4.**
(a) LTV = 20 × 0.75/0.05 = **$300**; LTV/CAC = **3.0**; payback = 100/(20 × 0.75) = **6.7 months** [5].
(b) Runway = 150,000/25,000 = **6 months** [2].
(c) Post-money = 1.5 + 0.5 = **$2.0 million**; share = 0.5/2.0 = **25 percent** [3].
